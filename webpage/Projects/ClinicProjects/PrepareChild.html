PREPARE CHILD – AUH

Can lifestyle changes, including a weight loss before pregnancy, prevent overweight in children?

Participant information

Eligible participants:

Couples planning a subsequent pregnancy within the next 3 years, metting the following criteria:

Women

  • Age 18–38 years
  • Pre-pregnancy BMI between 27,0-44,9 kg/m2
  • Registered to give birth at Aarhus University Hospital or Viborg Regional Hospital

Men

  • Age 18-55 år
  • BMI between 18,5-44,9 kg/m2

Project status

  • The project was initiated in June 2023.
  • 62 of 140 participants have been recruited (last updated 23.09.25)

    • Aim

    Prepare Child AUH is a research project examining how a lifestyle intervention, including weight loss before conception, affects the overall health of the prospective parents, the course of the pregnancy and the health of the child. The intervention takes place between two pregnancies, with participants recruited during their first pregnancy. The primary objective is to investigate whether weight loss achieved before a subsequent pregnancy has a beneficial impact on child health, assessed through measures such as body fat mass, epigenetics, and a range of additional biological and clinical parameters. Findings will be compared to both siblings and children whose parents did not participate in the lifestyle intervention.
