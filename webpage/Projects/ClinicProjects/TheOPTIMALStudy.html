The OPTIMAL Study

Optimizing follow-up after pregnancies affected by gestational diabetes

Participant information

Eligible participants:

The women included in this study are recruited from an established cohort of 1270 women diagnosed with GDM between 2019 and 2022 in the Central Denmark Region as part of a clinical follow-up program.

Project status

  • Project start December 2024.
  • 22 women are included (last updated 27.11.25).

    • Aim of the project

    The OPTIMAL study aims to improve follow-up care for women who have had gestational diabetes, as they are at increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes later in life. In the 5-year follow-up, 1270 women from an established cohort are invited to participate. By collecting blood and urine samples and questionnaire data, we aim to find out how many develop prediabetes or type 2 diabetes within five years, highlighting the importance of an optimal follow-up program after GDM. Furthermore, we seek to identify the most optimal test to diagnose type 2 diabetes after pregnancy and evaluate the communication between hospitals and general practitioners, as both are expected to influence follow-up rates following GDM pregnancies.
