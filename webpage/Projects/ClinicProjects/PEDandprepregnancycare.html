Prepregnancy Care for Women with Type 1 Diabetes and Type 2 Diabetes

Can targeted and individualized counseling before pregnancy lower blood glucose levels early in pregnancy in women with Type 1 Diabetes and Type 2 Diabetes?

Participant information

Eligible participants:

  • Women
  • Age 18-45 years
  • Type 1 Diabetes or Type 2 Diabetes
  • Pregnancy wish

Project status

  • The project was initiated in October 2024.
  • Recruitment is ongoing)

    • Aim

    During a two-years inclusion period we provide pre-pregnancy care for eligible women. We will examine whether pre-pregnancy care has beneficial effects on the following outcomes compared to women with diabetes who did not attend pre-pregnancy care:

    • HbA1c in 1st, 2nd and 3rd trimester
    • Gestational age at first visit at clinic for diabetic pregnancies
    • Pre-pregnancy use of folic acid
    • Use of potential harmful medication
    • Use of contraception
