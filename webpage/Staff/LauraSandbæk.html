Laura Sandbæk Andersen

Research year student

Laura is a medical student doing a research year at Aarhus University and Steno Diabetes Centre Aarhus. Her research aims to investigate what underlying rationales proceeds whether and how follow-up of individuals with prior gestational diabetes or preeclampsia are conducted in general practices. The project is a part of “The OPTIMAL study” that aims to improve follow-up in individuals with prior gestational diabetes, hich can contribute to prevention and early detection of type 2 diabetes which is a common complication.
