Line Sahlholdt Hansen

Ph.D. student

Line is a medical doctor and Ph.D. student at Aarhus University and Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus. In her PhD project “Optimizing follow-up after pregnancies affected by gestational diabetes – The OPTIMAL study”, she hopes to contribute to improving the follow-up of women with previous gestational diabetes, thereby enabling earlier detection of conditions such as type 2 diabetes, which is a common complication.
