Nanna Husted Jensen

MSc Public Health, PhD, Postdoc, Department of Public Health, Aarhus University

Nanna Husted holds a degree in Public Health Science and a PhD in Health Sciences. She is affiliated with the Department of Public Health at Aarhus University and collaborates closely with Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus. Her research focuses on overweight and obesity during pregnancy, gestational diabetes, and health behaviour interventions before, during and after pregnancy. She has expertise in complex intervention research and a particular interest in promoting health behaviours in the family setting before, during, and after pregnancy. She is currently involved in the LiPO Teen study, which aims to prevent overweight among children of mothers with overweight during pregnancy through investigation of the long-term effects of a lifestyle intervention during pregnancy.
