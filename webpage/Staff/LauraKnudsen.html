Laura Løftgaard Knudsen

Ph.D. student

Laura is a medical doctor and Ph.D. student at Aarhus University and Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus. In her PhD project “Insulin resistance in pregnancy and the effects on offspring health”, she aims to contribute to a better understanding of maternal insulin resistance during pregnancy and its impact on offspring health by investigating risk factors and metabolic markers of insulin resistance across different metabolic states.
