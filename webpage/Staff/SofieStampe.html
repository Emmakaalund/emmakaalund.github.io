Sofie Riise Stampe

Ph.D. student

Sofie is a medical doctor and a Ph.D. student at Aarhus University and Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus. Her research focuses on the use of whey protein as a non-pharmacological treatment for women with gestational diabetes. She will be conducting two clinical trials: The first is a randomized study that will focus on whey protein compared to insulin as treatment for GDM. The second will investigate the acute effects of whey protein on gastric emptying in women with GDM.
