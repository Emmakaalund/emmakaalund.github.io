THE PADME RESEARCH GROUP

PADME (Pregnancy And Diabetes Metabolism Epidemiology) is a research group anchored at Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus established in close collaboration with the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Aarhus University Hospital. We share a strong passion for supporting pregnant women living with metabolic diseases, with a commitment to improving health outcomes for both mother and child. Through dedication and collaboration we strive to better understand the challenges associated with metabolic conditions during pregnancy and to promote optimal care throughout this important period. Our goal is to contribute to healthier pregnancies, positive birth outcomes, and long-term well-being for both mothers and their children.


Improving pregnancies complicated by diabetes and metabolic disease

Ambition

Our ambition is that women with diabetes and other metabolic diseases can achieve a pregnancy that is as successful and uncomplicated as healthy women.

Mission

We have a shared mission to know more about how enviromental and metabolic changes such as diabetes and obesity affect pregnancy, fetus, mother and child in the short and long term, and want our research to benefit future parents and their children and thus the next generation.

Foundation

Based at Aarhus University Hospital in Aarhus, Denmark, our research group benefits from close integration with the clinical environment, enabling us to identify clinically relevant research questions and translate clinical needs into impactful research. Our strong collaboration with the Department of Clinical Medicine at Aarhus University further strengthens the link between clinical practice and academic research. We are an interdisciplinary research group that brings together diverse expertise and complementary competencies to foster scientific collaboration and advance research. In parallel, we are committed to cultivating a supportive, safe, and inclusive research environment that promotes the professional development of early-career researchers and enables them to thrive within the academic community
